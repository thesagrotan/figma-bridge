Explore without losing the original.

Select a component in Figma. PARALLEL inspects its structure, proposes distinct directions, renders each one, and keeps your source component untouched while you decide. Add a written brief to steer the exploration.

Optional. PARALLEL maps the brief to transformations it actually supports — it never invents edits. Without an AI provider it uses a built-in parser.
